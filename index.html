Any and All Sports

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Any and All Sports ("the app") displays publicly available sports schedules and broadcast information. We built it to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not require an account, does not ask for personal information, and does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to us.

What the app accesses

  • Schedule data: the app downloads daily sports schedule files from a public content repository so it can show what games are on.
  • Local storage: recently viewed schedules are cached on your device only, to make the app faster. This cache never leaves your device.
  • Reminders (optional): if you enable game reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. We do not receive any notification of this.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Apple may process standard system-level information (such as crash logs) under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, it will be updated on this page.
